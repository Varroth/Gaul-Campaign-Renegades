# Abandoned Dead — Vephar, the Uncrowned Sovereign — Chapter Master v1.9 (playtest)

**Published as a Vephar-only update** to the existing 59-entry Abandoned Dead catalogue, under Gaul Campaign: Renegades game-system revision 7. All other 58 Abandoned Dead units, Infernal Warriors, and Frozen Cogs data remain unchanged.

**Canonical title:** Vephar, the Uncrowned Sovereign. His formal office remains Chapter Master; historical "Chaos Lord" references are not his current title.

## Approved Chapter Master kit and profile
- Vephar is openly recognized by his captains, Triarchate, and Elias Veyr. Alpha Legion / Alpharius-inspired external deception, not internal confusion.
- M 6", T 5, Sv 2+, W **7**, Ld 5+, OC **4**, 4+ invulnerable.
- **The Veiled Truth**, permanent master-crafted power halberd. Cleave: A6, WS2+, S8, AP-3, D3. Sweep: A10, WS2+, S5, AP-2, D1. Choose only one each time he fights.
- Ranged choose exactly one: default **The False Witness** with Bolter Volley (24", A3, BS2+, S5, AP-1, D2, Assault), or one Precision Execution Round (24", A1, BS2+, S6, AP-2, D3, Precision, once per battle **instead of** Bolter Volley); **OR** Master-crafted Plasma Pistol (12", A1, BS2+, S8, AP-3, D2, Pistol, Hazardous). 
- **220 points provisional**, formerly 175.

## Five activated abilities during battle
1. **The Unbroken Chain** (each friendly Command phase): select up to two friendly Abandoned Dead Infantry units within 12"; one Wound reroll each when selected to shoot or fight in a phase, until your next Command phase.
2. **The Serpent Changes Course** (once/battle, your Movement phase): one nearby friendly Infantry unit that has completed a Normal Move may move up to 6" more, but cannot Advance or Charge that turn. No free Hidden.
3. **Coordinated Decapitation** (once/battle, start of your Shooting phase): select a visible enemy within 18", then up to two friendly Infantry units within 12"; +1 Hit against that enemy until phase end, standard modifier cap.
4. **Ghost-Command Misdirection** (once/battle, enemy Shooting phase): when a nearby Infantry unit is targeted, it gains Cover and opposing attacks cannot reroll Hit rolls against it for the phase; no automatic Hidden.
5. **The Veiled Truth Unmasked** (once/battle, when Vephar fights): halberd Cleave gains Precision and Lethal Hits **or** Sweep gains Sustained Hits 1 for that Fight phase.

Ghost-Shroud remains a passive standard Chapter rule and is **not** counted as one of these five activated abilities.
