## Culture
The Rite of the Bound Tide renews the Chapter's oath of fealty to the Frozen Cogs; it is an obligation, not an alliance of equals. The Storm Council interprets omens, but the High Stormseer commands. Oathkeepers maintain ancestral sea-raider customs and enforce psychic restraint. Battle-brothers mark armour with angular tidal and lightning motifs, recording earned honors rather than arbitrary mutations. Tzeentch worship centers on mastery of change, foresight and disciplined psychic knowledge. Visible mutation is subject to containment and correction rather than celebration; dangerously unstable psykers are isolated pending specialist assessment. The Chapter's ancestral culture predates its fall.
