# Silver Tempests — Gual Campaign establishment

## Canon
Former Steeltide Bulwarks renegades, Tzeentch-aligned, ancient Pictish-inspired maritime warrior culture. Silver and blue armour; elongated eye with four blue lightning bolts. Absolute subordination to Warsmith Bheulach of the Frozen Cogs.
Approximately 250 renegades survived the original civil war; rebuilt to 900 active Astartes by the Gual Campaign. At the disturbance only 250 were near Gual and able to deploy. The other 650 remained temporarily trapped in the Eye of Terror awaiting predictable warp passage. This is not attrition or permanent separation. Dreadnoughts and mortal crews are recorded separately. Recruitment and training doctrine is deferred.

## Command

Ten Storm Captains, 15 Tide Lieutenants, 36 company Stormseers (10 High Tide, 16 qualified, 10 Adepts), 55 support specialists (15 Apothecaries, 20 Techmarines, 10 Oathkeepers, 10 signals/logistics), 714 other company personnel, 70 central personnel. Total 900.

## Psychic doctrine
Every Astartes has psychic potential; sergeants are trained practitioners. Dedicated specialists advance through graduated mastery. Five disciplines: Tempestcraft, Tidewarding, Farsight, Ironweaving, Mindweaving. Conventional Astartes durability; no Infernal Warriors regeneration or enhanced wounds.
