Carbines match the catalogue's existing Lascarbine baseline. Sidearms match its Laspistol baseline. Knives use modest mortal close-combat statistics. These are conventional weapons without Psychic, Anti-Psyker, Precision or additional null effects. Apply the normal Pistol shooting restriction; the sidearm does not add an extra attack to carbine shooting.

## Fixed equipment

- Independent Nullifier Operator and team primary operator: Compact Carbine, Sidearm, Combat Knife.
- Both technicians: Directorate Carbine and Sidearm; field-maintenance equipment remains non-weapon equipment.
- Both optional security operatives: Directorate Carbine, Sidearm, Combat Knife.

Each weapon is a compulsory, default-selected equipment entry with a complete exported Weapon profile. There are no replacement choices, additional modes or extra weapon costs. The null-field arrays remain support equipment.

Existing costs are retained: independent Operator 35 points; team 50 points for its three required models, plus 10 points per optional security operative (60/70 for four/five models). These remain homebrew costs requiring playtesting. This update changes no null-field ranges, psychic suppression rules, model statistics, unit identifiers or attachment permissions.

## Validation and limits

All six model slots, including optional team members, are checked for exact fixed loadouts, compulsory defaults, all seven profile fields and absence of extra weapon costs. The existing minimum-model/points checks remain in place. All 24 regression tests pass locally, along with full-catalogue structural/reference audits and manifest verification.

The earlier maintenance report's missing-weapon-profile limitation is resolved by this release. Native attachments, Psychic weapon interactions and device automatic refresh still require separate validation. No native New Recruit execution is claimed by the static tests.
