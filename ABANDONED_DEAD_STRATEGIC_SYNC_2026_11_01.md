## Approved core identity (preserve)
Renegade Space Marine Chapter, not a Chaos-worshipping Chapter. Vephar, the Uncrowned Sovereign, is Chapter Master. The Triarchate consists of Davask Ghorum, Porox Toramini and Ossus Korpanos. Shadow Director Elias Veyr leads the professional mortal Liberation Directorate. Astartes conventional forces, Directorate-heavy formations and mixed task forces are all valid without a mandatory mixed-force tax.

## Approved operational framework
- Six intended detachments: conventional Astartes, combined arms, Directorate operations, reconnaissance/sabotage, armored warfare, siege/defensive.
- Layered defense; combined anti-armor; integrated reconnaissance; flexible combined-arms command; flexible expeditionary warfare; integrated sustainment; integrated fire support; veteran combined-arms identity; coordinated territorial control.
- Reclaimed arsenal, extensive role-based customization, controlled xenos adaptation, and integrated reclamation/engineering authority.
- Ghost-Shroud passive suppression applies to Astartes; specialized modules are limited and must be defined on eligible units.
- Strategic doctrines 28–30: integrated naval expeditionary operations, protected territorial sovereignty, unified expeditionary warfare.
- Unified Forces with Infernal Warriors and Frozen Cogs is a separate standing, consent-based homebrew coalition option.
