FIELD MANUAL

How to play

This alpha is a streamlined tactical board game inspired by tabletop wargaming, not an exact Warhammer 40,000 rules engine.

01

Build your forces

Open Army Builder, add squads and characters on both sides, then start a new battle. The starter armies are approximately 1,000 simulator points each.

02

Movement

Select a friendly unit, then click an unoccupied reachable tile. Walls and other units block paths. Each squad moves once per turn.

03

Shooting and charging

Click Next phase to enter shooting; select your unit then an enemy in range. In charge phase, select an attacker and an enemy to attempt a 2D6 charge.

04

Fight and score

During fight phase, attack adjacent enemies. After each round-two-or-later turn, score 5 VP per controlled objective. Model count × OC determines control.

05

End the round

After fight phase, advance to let the opponent play automatically. The battle ends after five rounds or when a force is destroyed.

06

Save and test

Save/load in this browser, export/import JSON, and run ten-game automated tests. Seeds reproduce the same outcomes with the same armies and engine version.

Alpha rules and known limitations

Profiles and points are demonstrators, not imports from the live New Recruit catalogues. Combat uses D6 hit, wound, armour save, individual damage allocation and optional Feel No Pain. Terrain cover modifies saving throws; line of sight, stratagems, command points, full psychic tests, transport rules, reinforcement rules, model-by-model movement, detailed Leader attachments, and faction detachment rules are not yet implemented.

Infernal Warriors receive simplified 5+ Feel No Pain and one wound of start-of-turn recovery. Abandoned Dead Ghost-Shroud increases required ranged Hit rolls at long range. A nearby Abandoned Dead Nullifier makes simulated psychic shooting less accurate. Silver Tempests psychic mastery is represented by ranged attacks; Frozen Cogs containment has not been modeled. These simplifications are explicitly not final faction rules.
