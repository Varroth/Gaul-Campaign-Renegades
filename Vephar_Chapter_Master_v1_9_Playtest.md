# Vephar, the Uncrowned Sovereign — Chapter Master of the Abandoned Dead
## Playtest Datasheet v1.9 — Five activated battle abilities

**Canonical epithet:** The Uncrowned Sovereign. **Formal office:** Chapter Master of the Abandoned Dead. The historical designation "Chaos Lord" is not his current canonical title.

Vephar is visibly and reliably known to his Chapter's captains, the Triarchate and Elias Veyr. His operational deception is directed at his enemies. He is the first successful hybrid Astartes of the Abandoned Dead, not Alpharius or an Alpha Legion genetic descendant. Some older sources refer to him as **Chaos Lord**; this is a historical descriptor, not his canonical title or an assertion of Chaos worship.

**Characteristic profile:** M 6", T 5, Sv 2+, W **7**, Ld 5+, OC **4**; 4+ invulnerable save; passive approved Ghost-Shroud. **220 points, provisional.** Unique Character (maximum one).

## Five battle-active abilities

### 1. The Unbroken Chain — Command Phase

EACH OF YOUR COMMAND PHASES: Select up to two friendly ABANDONED DEAD INFANTRY units within 12" of Vephar (including his own unit). Until the start of your next Command phase, once in each phase when each selected unit is selected to shoot or fight, that unit may re-roll one Wound roll made for one of its attacks. A dice roll cannot be re-rolled more than once.

### 2. The Serpent Changes Course — Movement Phase

ONCE PER BATTLE, YOUR MOVEMENT PHASE: Immediately after one friendly ABANDONED DEAD INFANTRY unit within 12" of Vephar finishes a Normal Move, it can make an additional Normal Move of up to 6". The chosen unit cannot Advance or declare a charge this turn. It must obey normal movement and Engagement Range restrictions. This move does not automatically confer Hidden.

### 3. Coordinated Decapitation — Shooting Phase

ONCE PER BATTLE, AT THE START OF YOUR SHOOTING PHASE: Select one enemy unit visible to Vephar and within 18" of him; then select up to two friendly ABANDONED DEAD INFANTRY units within 12" of Vephar (his own unit may be selected). Until the end of this phase, add 1 to Hit rolls for attacks those selected friendly units make against that enemy. The normal cumulative Hit modifier limit applies. This is one use for the army, not one use per selected unit.

### 4. Ghost-Command Misdirection — Enemy Shooting Phase

ONCE PER BATTLE, DURING YOUR OPPONENT'S SHOOTING PHASE: When a friendly ABANDONED DEAD INFANTRY unit within 12" of Vephar is selected as the target of an enemy unit's ranged attacks, declare this ability before any Hit rolls. Until the end of that phase, the chosen friendly unit has the Benefit of Cover against ranged attacks, and enemy models cannot re-roll Hit rolls for ranged attacks that target it. This does not automatically make the unit Hidden or stack additional cover benefits.

### 5. The Veiled Truth Unmasked — Fight Phase

ONCE PER BATTLE, WHEN VEPHAR IS SELECTED TO FIGHT: Before resolving any of his attacks, choose the Cleave or Sweep profile of The Veiled Truth. Until the end of that Fight phase, Cleave gains [PRECISION] and [LETHAL HITS], OR Sweep gains [SUSTAINED HITS 1], depending on the chosen profile. This applies only to Vephar's halberd attacks. Do not attack with both profiles in one fight.

## Attachments and rule compatibility

Vephar may attach to Ghostline Squad, Blackout Breachers, Pallbearer Veterans or Veil Stalker Hunter-Killer Squad. No more than one Leader per unit; attached squad keeps its own Sergeant and weapon limits. Vephar's active effects never automatically grant Hidden, bypass target legality, provide duplicate identical bonuses or affect units outside **Abandoned Dead**. Ghost-Shroud is a **separate existing passive rule**, not a sixth activated ability.

## Balance status

Playtest implementation only. The jump from 175 to 220 points reflects seven Wounds, a stronger halberd and the five tactical abilities. Quantitative game balance, mission-based playtests, and New Recruit UI/roster export have **not** been independently verified. The previously approved Chapter and mortal rules are unchanged; only Vephar's datasheet and his selectable loadout are revised.
