Quick rules reminders

Infernal Serum: Eligible living inducted Astartes have FNP 5+ and recover 1 lost wound at the start of their turn; no serum for Mortal Auxilia, Forge Servitors or summoned Daemons. Check the codex's eligible +1 Wound profiles.

Lost Cohort: Maximum two squads combined. Each has a separate, paid, qualifying attached Chaplain in the included formation costs; if a Chaplain is lost, follow control test and 12-inch supervision rules. Cohort Insertion follows the agreed homebrew first-round exception.

Bloodletter Invocation: Once per battle per army. Paid BLOODLETTERS must already be in legal Reserves; one non-Battle-shocked Thralls unit on the table may place them wholly within 6 inches and more than 9 inches from enemies, obeying normal Reserve timing and placement restrictions.

Offline preflight only. This is not New Recruit's native validation engine; it cannot calculate occupancy across several unlinked transports, resolve native Leader associations or make on-table judgments. It never rewrites roster files.
